M-Pesa payment instructions

After submitting your booking, select Lipa na M-Pesa → Buy Goods and Services → Till Number 4831168. Enter the fare applicable to your journey and keep the M-Pesa confirmation message. This independent website does not automatically verify M-Pesa payments.
