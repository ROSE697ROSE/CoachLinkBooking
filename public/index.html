How It Works

Three simple steps to submit your booking

1

Choose your journey

Enter your departure, destination and travel date.

2

Submit booking

Log in to your passenger account and submit your journey details.

3

Pay by M-Pesa

Approve the M-Pesa prompt on your phone and wait for automatic payment confirmation.

Secure M-Pesa payments

When your route fare and Daraja merchant account are configured, CoachLink will send an M-Pesa STK prompt to your phone. Enter your PIN and wait for automatic payment confirmation. Do not send money to an unverified Till number.

Print Ticket

After payment is confirmed, your electronic ticket and QR code will appear automatically. Log in to your passenger account to view it.

CoachLink is an independent bus booking service. Route schedules and fares are published only after verification by the operator.
