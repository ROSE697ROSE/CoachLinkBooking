Payment instructions

After submitting your booking, pay using M-Pesa: Lipa na M-Pesa → Buy Goods and Services → Till Number 4831168. Enter the correct fare for your journey and keep the M-Pesa confirmation message. Payments are not automatically verified by this website; ticket issuance follows payment verification.
