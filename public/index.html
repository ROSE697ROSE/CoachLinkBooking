How it works

1. Enter trip details

Choose your route, travel date and passenger count.

2. Submit request

Your request is saved in the online bookings database.

3. Await confirmation

The operator must confirm availability, fare and ticket details before payment.

Payment safety

Do not send money until an authorised ENA Coach representative confirms your booking and provides verified payment instructions. This site does not yet process or verify M-Pesa payments.

This is an independent booking-request website. It is not an official ENA Coach website. Routes, fares, schedules, seat availability and ticket issuance require confirmation by the operator.
